# 🚀 JobPilot AI

### Your AI Career Copilot

JobPilot AI is an AI-powered career assistant that helps students and job seekers find relevant jobs, understand skill gaps, track applications, and prepare for interviews.

**Resume → AI Analysis → Job Matching → Skill Gap → Apply → Interview**

---

## ✨ Features

* 📄 **AI Resume Analyzer** — Extracts skills, education, projects, and experience.
* 🤖 **AI Job Matching** — Compares your profile with job requirements.
* 💼 **Job Recommendations** — Suggests relevant opportunities.
* 📊 **Application Tracker** — Track applications from Saved → Applied → Interview → Offer.
* 📚 **Skill Gap Analyzer** — Identifies missing skills and learning areas.
* 🎤 **Interview Preparation** — Generates technical, project, and behavioral questions.
* 🧠 **AI Mock Interview** — Provides feedback on interview answers.
* ✍️ **Resume Improvement** — Improves resume content without fabricating experience.
* 💬 **Career Copilot** — AI assistant for career and job-search questions.

## 🌟 Why JobPilot AI?

Traditional job platforms mainly answer:

> **"What jobs are available?"**

JobPilot AI aims to answer:

> **"Which jobs are right for me, why do they match, what am I missing, and how can I prepare?"**
